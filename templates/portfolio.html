Build a portfolio from a backtest

Replays every filled signal from an as-of scan, sized against equity at the time of each trade, so wins compound and losses shrink the next position.

Options mode prices each contract from the CURRENT chain, not the chain that existed on the as-of date — Yahoo does not serve historical chains. Stock mode is the honest one for a historical replay.

Simulation only. Nothing is routed anywhere. Fills are assumed at the recorded price with no slippage or commission, and option marks come from Black–Scholes with flat IV rather than a live quote. Position sizing risks a fixed % of equity from entry to stop, capped at 20% of equity in any one position.
